# Pass: copy (headline, actions, labels)

Scope: every page (page text: browser tool `get_page_text`, or the screenshots). Category: Copy & microcopy.

- The headline says what it is and for whom, in the audience's words (plain for seniors and consumers, specific and technical for engineers).
- Action labels say what happens ("Book a free call", not "Submit"); errors say what went wrong and what to do next.
- Consistent terms across pages; no placeholders or lorem ipsum; consistent capitalization style.
- Names match across views: a card title, the detail page heading and what the item itself shows agree.
- Input placeholders and button labels fit their control at the smallest phone width (`responsive` pass measured truncation).
- Regulated industries (insurance, health, finance): required disclaimers present and readable (size, contrast, spacing; see `space-edges` for callouts touching edges), never in images.
